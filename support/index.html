Stuck? Most answers are below. If not, email hello@heyrighto.app and a real person will reply.

My alerts aren't arriving

Check these, in order:

  1. Notifications: iPhone Settings, Notifications, Righto. Turn on Allow Notifications and Time Sensitive Notifications.
  2. Focus: if you use a Focus (like Sleep or Work), add Righto to its allowed apps, or keep Time Sensitive Notifications on.
  3. Low Power Mode can delay traffic updates. Righto still sends your alerts, but the times may be less up to date.
  4. Open Righto once a day. iOS gives apps you use more time to refresh in the background.
Why did my leave time change?

Righto keeps checking the traffic. If the roads get busier, it moves your leave time earlier and tells you. If they clear, you get a few more minutes.

How does parking time work?

When you drive somewhere, Righto asks where you park. It adds the time to park and walk from the car park to the door, so you arrive at the actual door on time, not just the car park. Saved places remember your choice. Places you've only searched for ask each time.

Trains and buses

Choose "Train or bus" when you plan a trip. Righto uses Apple Maps' public transport times, so it works where Apple Maps has timetables. Check the operator for disruption on the day.

Sharing a trip with a friend

Open the trip and tap Share trip. Your friend gets a link. If they have Righto, it opens straight in the app and works out their own leave time from wherever they are. If they don't, the link shows the trip and how to get Righto. Changes you make after sharing aren't sent to them.

How do I delete my information?

Delete any trip or saved place in the app. To remove everything, delete Righto from your iPhone. Righto keeps nothing on a server, so that's all there is. See Privacy.

Does Righto track me?

No. Righto only uses your location while you're using it, keeps your trips on your iPhone, and has no account, ads or tracking.
