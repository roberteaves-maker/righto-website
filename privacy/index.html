Privacy

Short version: Righto keeps your information on your iPhone. There's no account, no tracking and no ads, and we never sell anything about you.

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Who we are

Righto is made by R Eaves Studios in the UK. If you have any question about your privacy, email hello@heyrighto.app.

What stays on your iPhone

Your trips, saved places (like Home and Work), getting-ready times, parking choices, your first name and your trip history are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. We don't have a server that receives them, and we can't see them. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, Apple includes this in your backup, like any other app.

What Righto asks permission for

Location (only while using the app)

Righto uses your current location as the starting point for a trip, so it can work out how long the journey takes and when you need to leave. It does not track you in the background.

Motion & Fitness

Righto uses this to notice when you start and stop driving, so it can log when you actually left and arrived and compare that with the plan. This stays on your iPhone.

Calendar

If you choose to, Righto reads your calendar so you can turn appointments into trips. Your calendar never leaves your iPhone. Righto doesn't change your calendar.

Notifications

Righto sends reminders such as "Time to start getting ready" and "Time to go". These are created on your iPhone, not sent from a server.

Contacts (no permission needed)

When you pick a friend's address, Righto opens Apple's contact picker. Righto only receives the one contact you pick (their address and first name), never the rest of your contacts.

Apple Maps

To search for places and work out journey times with live traffic, trains and buses, Righto uses Apple Maps. That means the places you search for and the start and end of each journey are sent to Apple to get the route. Apple doesn't link this to your Apple Account. See Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing a trip

If you share a trip, Righto makes a link containing only the place's name and address, its map position, the arrive-by date and time, any short note you add and your first name. It never includes your home, where you're leaving from, your leave time, your route or your history.

The trip details sit in the part of the link after the "#", which web browsers never send to a website. So when a friend opens your link, our website doesn't receive the trip, and it isn't stored anywhere. You choose who to send it to, using Messages, WhatsApp or any app you like.

Purchases

Any purchase in Righto is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Righto has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't share or sell any information about you. If you've turned on "Share With App Developers" in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can turn that off in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

This website

heyrighto.app uses no cookies and no analytics. The only thing it sends to another company is your email address, and only if you join the waitlist. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep visitors' IP addresses in its security logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The waitlist

If you join the Righto waitlist on this website, we keep your email address so we can tell you when Righto is out, and now and then send Righto news. That's all we use it for, and we only email you if you confirm your sign-up. The list is kept by our email service, Kit (ConvertKit), which stores it for us and doesn't use it for anything else. See Kit's privacy policy.

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email hello@heyrighto.app to be removed. When you unsubscribe, we delete your address. Joining the waitlist is completely separate from the app: the app never sees your email.

Deleting your information

You can delete any trip or saved place in the app. Deleting Righto from your iPhone deletes everything it stored. We hold nothing from the app about you. If you joined the waitlist, unsubscribing deletes your email address.

Children

Righto isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. The app keeps everything on your iPhone, so the only thing we might hold is your email address if you joined the waitlist. Just ask at hello@heyrighto.app. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
